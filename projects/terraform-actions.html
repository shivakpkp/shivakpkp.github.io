Reusable Terraform delivery

A shared set of composite actions for consistent Terraform setup, planning, deployment, and destroy planning.

TerraformGitHub ActionsAzure Storage
01

The challenge

Standardize repeated Terraform steps across infrastructure repositories.

02

My contribution

I separated setup, initialization and provider caching from plan and apply responsibilities. Saved plans pass between jobs as artifacts, with change detection available to the caller.

03

Engineering decisions

The calling workflow owns permissions, environment approvals and concurrency. Applying a saved plan keeps the deployment tied to the reviewed plan; initialization restores the backend and providers on a new runner.

04

What this demonstrates

A reusable delivery foundation with documented inputs, outputs and caller examples.
